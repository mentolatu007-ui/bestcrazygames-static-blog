Simple Habits That Improve Your Timing

A few small habits can make a big difference. Try to read the full task list before touching anything. Then group tasks that share a similar timing window. Avoid rushing the first action, because a slow start often prevents a messy middle. After each attempt, take a second to notice where the chain broke. That quick review turns every loss into useful information for the next try.
